📖 How to Play

🎰 Spin & Match

Press SPIN to roll the reels. Reels decelerate and stop one by one.

  • 2 of a kind = Small Match (Points + Coins)
  • 3 of a kind = Match (High Points)
  • 4 of a kind = Super Match (+Combo Multiplier!)
  • 5 of a kind = Mega Match (+Screen Shake & Mega Bonus!)

✨ Special Symbols

🌈Wild

Substitutes for any normal symbol.

🌟Super Wild

Matches any symbol with extra bonus points.

💣Bomb

Detonates and converts adjacent symbols.

❓Mystery

Reveals a high-tier symbol upon stopping.

🔒Locked

Must be matched to crack open.

✖️2Multiplier

Doubles all win scores on this spin.
